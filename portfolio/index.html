01 Personalized spaces CHI 2026

Roomify

Reimagine your room. Keep its spatial reality.

An AI-driven system that transforms physical rooms into themed virtual environments while preserving their layout and furniture functions. A cross-reality authoring tool lets users refine the space in MR and experience it in VR.

02 Multisensory communication IEEE TVCG 2025

VR Whispering

Bring the feeling of a whisper into social VR.

A private conversation technique combining avatar proximity, whisper-like audio, and gentle airflow near the ear. These coordinated cues help people recognize private exchanges and feel connected, while a contextual record keeps them in touch with the surrounding conversation.

  • Social VR
  • Multisensory interaction
  • Privacy experience

03 Usable security IEEE ISMAR 2025

cLock

One hand. Two factors. An interaction made for VR.

A single-handed authentication system that turns wrist rotation and finger tapping into PIN input on a circular interface. It combines the PIN with behavioral biometrics captured during the same interaction, balancing usability and resistance to shoulder surfing.

  • Gesture interaction
  • Behavioral biometrics
  • VR authentication
